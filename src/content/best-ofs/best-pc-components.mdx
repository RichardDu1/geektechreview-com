## How We Ranked These PC Components & Peripherals

PC components and peripherals live or die by daily-use quality: sustained throughput for storage and docks, sensor accuracy and latency for mice, switch feel and wireless stability for keyboards, and color/response for monitors. We benchmark each on our 0–10 scale using Methodology v1.0, weighting reliability and real-world performance over spec-sheet peaks.

All seven picks here are devices we've fully reviewed, ranked by score and how well each serves its purpose.

## Our Top Picks

A clever, beautifully machined aluminum sleeve that slips under the Mac Mini and adds the ports you actually need — all without cluttering your desk. It's the accessory that makes the Mac Mini feel complete. [Read the full review →](/reviews/pc-components/satechi-mac-mini-hub/)

### 2. Samsung T9 Portable SSD — Best Portable SSD

Fast enough to edit video directly off of, tough enough to toss in a bag, and reliable enough to trust with a project. The T9 saturates USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 and is the external drive we recommend without hesitation. [Read the full review →](/reviews/pc-components/samsung-t9-portable-ssd/)

### 3. Razer Viper V3 Pro — Best Gaming Mouse

Ultralight without feeling cheap, with a flawless sensor and a shape that works for claw, palm, and fingertip grips. If you take aiming seriously, this is the mouse. [Read the full review →](/reviews/pc-components/razer-viper-v3-pro/)

### 4. CalDigit TS4 — Best Thunderbolt Dock

One cable to connect your entire desk. Eighteen ports, enough power delivery to charge a laptop, and the stability CalDigit is known for. The dock that ends dongle fatigue. [Read the full review →](/reviews/pc-components/caldigit-ts4/)

The MX Master line keeps getting better. The 4 refines the ergonomic shape, adds a faster MagSpeed wheel, and ships with the best multi-device software in the business. The default for knowledge workers and creatives. [Read the full review →](/reviews/pc-components/logitech-mx-master-4/)
