## How We Ranked This Audio Gear

Audio gear is judged on what matters for each form factor: noise cancellation strength, sound signature and detail, comfort over long sessions, battery life, microphone quality, and value. We A/B test headphones and earbuds against reference tracks and measure ANC attenuation — every score reflects Methodology v1.0 testing.

All five picks here are devices we've fully reviewed, ranked by overall score and category fit.

## Our Top Picks

### 1. Sony WF-1000XM5 Earbuds — Best Overall / Best Earbuds

True wireless has finally caught up to over-ear ANC. The XM5 earbuds deliver noise cancellation that rivals full-size headphones, a comfortable all-day fit, and warm, detailed sound — all in a pocketable case. For most people this is the smartest audio purchase of 2026. [Read the full review →](/reviews/audio/sony-wf-1000xm5/)

### 2. Sonos Arc 2 — Best Soundbar

If you're building a home theater, the Arc 2 is the centerpiece. Wide, tall Dolby Atmos soundstage, automatic TruePlay room tuning, and rock-solid Sonos multi-room integration. Pair it with a sub and surrounds for a system that punches well above its price. [Read the full review →](/reviews/audio/sonos-arc-2/)

### 3. Sony WH-1000XM6 — Best Over-Ear Headphones

The default recommendation for over-ear ANC hasn't changed: the XM6 offers the best noise cancellation you can buy, plush all-day comfort, and 30 hours of battery. It's a boring upgrade over the XM5, but if you don't already own a pair, these are the ones to get. [Read the full review →](/reviews/audio/sony-wh-1000xm6/)

### 4. Shure SM7dB — Best Microphone for Podcasting & Streaming

The legendary SM7B voice, now with an active preamp built in — no more Cloudlifter required. It's the broadcast standard for a reason: flattering on every voice, rejects room noise beautifully, and is built like a tank. A buy-once mic for serious creators. [Read the full review →](/reviews/audio/shure-sm7db/)

### 5. Bose QuietComfort Ultra — Best for Comfort

Where Sony wins on pure ANC, Bose wins on comfort and the immersive spatial-audio experience. If you wear headphones for 6+ hours a day or prioritize a plush, pressure-free fit, the QC Ultra is the better choice over the XM6. [Read the full review →](/reviews/audio/bose-qc-ultra-headphones/)
