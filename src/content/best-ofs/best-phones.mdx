## How We Ranked These Phones & Wearables

We test phones and wearables across the metrics that matter day-to-day: real-world app and gaming performance, sustained battery drain under mixed use, display brightness and color accuracy, camera quality across lighting conditions, and durability. Each device is scored on our 0–10 scale using Methodology v1.0.

The Apple Watch Ultra 3 is the only device in this category we've fully reviewed so far — it anchors the list. We've added the top market picks we're actively testing to cover the flagship phone and everyday-smartwatch gaps.

## Our Top Picks

### 1. Apple Watch Ultra 3 — Best Rugged Smartwatch

A titanium build, the brightest Apple Watch display, multi-day battery in low-power mode, and precision dual-frequency GPS make this the smartwatch for ultramarathoners, divers, and backcountry types. For everyone else it's more watch than you need — the standard Apple Watch Series model is the better buy. [Read the full review →](/reviews/phones/apple-watch-ultra-3/)

### 2. Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra — Best Android Phone

Flat display, anti-glare glass, a best-in-class Snapdragon chip, and deep Galaxy AI integration. The S25 Ultra is the most complete Android flagship, and the S Pen still sets it apart for note-takers and creatives.

### 3. Apple iPhone 16 Pro — Best iPhone

The 16 Pro hits the sweet spot: flagship A18 Pro performance, a genuinely useful Camera Control, excellent battery, and a size that's still manageable. Unless you need the Ultra-class telephoto zoom or the biggest screen, this is the iPhone to buy.

### 4. Google Pixel Watch 3 — Best Wear OS Watch

Clean software, Fitbit health features baked in, and a brighter, thinner-bezel round display than its predecessor. The most polished Android smartwatch for everyday wear and fitness tracking.

### 5. Google Pixel 9 Pro — Best Camera Phone

If photography is your priority, the Pixel 9 Pro's computational camera consistently produces the most natural, detailed shots — especially in challenging light. The compact Pro size is a bonus for one-handed use.
