## How We Ranked This Gaming Gear

Gaming gear is scored on the experience it delivers: input latency, display response and refresh, sustained performance, build and comfort, game library, and value. We test displays with real game patterns and VR headsets across extended sessions. Every score follows Methodology v1.0.

The LG G4 OLED and Meta Quest 3 are our fully reviewed anchors. We've added the top market picks we're testing to cover the handheld, console, and controller gaps.

## Our Top Picks

### 1. LG G4 OLED TV — Best Gaming Display

For gaming, nothing beats a fast OLED. The G4 delivers perfect blacks, 144Hz variable refresh rate over HDMI 2.1, and near-instant pixel response with no motion blur. HDR games look better here than on displays costing multiples more. If a TV is your gaming screen, this is the one. [Read the full review →](/reviews/gaming/lg-g4-oled/)

### 2. Meta Quest 3 — Best VR Headset

Standalone VR done right: a sharp, wide color passthrough for mixed-reality apps, a much faster chip than the Quest 2, and a deep content library with no PC required. The lowest-friction way to get into VR, and a great fitness and social platform to boot. [Read the full review →](/reviews/gaming/meta-quest-3/)

### 3. Steam Deck OLED — Best Handheld

Valve's OLED refresh fixed the original's one weakness — the screen — and added better battery life. SteamOS is the most console-like PC gaming experience, and your existing Steam library just works. The best handheld for most PC gamers.

### 4. PlayStation 5 Pro — Best Console

If you want maximum fidelity on the couch, the PS5 Pro delivers upscaled 4K with credible ray tracing and a quiet, premium build. The pick for Sony's first-party lineup and the best-looking console exclusives of 2026.

### 5. Xbox Wireless Controller — Best Gamepad

Don't overthink controllers. The Xbox Wireless pad remains the most comfortable, reliable gamepad across Xbox, PC, and mobile, at a price that leaves room for games. The sensible default that beats most "pro" controllers on ergonomics.
