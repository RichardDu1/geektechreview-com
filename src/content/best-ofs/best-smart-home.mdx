## How We Ranked This Smart Home Gear

Smart home devices are judged on reliability above all: how consistently they respond, how well the app and automations work, ecosystem compatibility (Matter, HomeKit, Alexa, Google), and the real-world usefulness of their headline feature. We test each in a live home network over weeks. Every score follows Methodology v1.0.

All four picks here are devices we've fully reviewed.

## Our Top Picks

### 1. Eve Energy Matter Plug — Best Smart Plug

Smart plugs are commodity items, but the Eve Energy stands out: native Matter and Thread support means it responds locally and fast, works across every major ecosystem, and tracks energy use accurately. The plug that just works — and works everywhere. [Read the full review →](/reviews/smart-home/eve-energy-matter/)

### 2. Ring Battery Doorbell Pro — Best Video Doorbell

Crisp HDR video day and night, fast and accurate person detection, and the unmatched Ring ecosystem (neighbors, cloud recording, quick replies). Battery power means no wiring — drop it on and go. The front-door security pick. [Read the full review →](/reviews/smart-home/ring-battery-doorbell-pro/)

### 3. Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra — Best Robot Vacuum

Top-tier suction, a vibrating sonic mop that actually scrubs stains, obstacle avoidance smart enough to dodge cables and pet waste, and a dock that empties the bin, washes the mop, and refills water. Set it and genuinely forget it. [Read the full review →](/reviews/smart-home/roborock-s8-maxv-ultra/)

### 4. Aqara U100 Smart Lock — Best Smart Lock (Apple Home)

Fingerprint, keypad, and Apple Home Key unlock — tap your phone or watch to get in — with Thread and Matter for future-proofing. The best lock if you're invested in HomeKit/Apple Home, though the verdict lands at "wait" while software maturity catches up to the hardware. [Read the full review →](/reviews/smart-home/aqara-u100-smart-lock/)
