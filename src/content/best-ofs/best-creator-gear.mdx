## How We Ranked This Creator Gear

Creator gear is judged on what it lets you make: image and video quality, usability and ergonomics in the field, build and weather resistance, ecosystem and software, and value. We shoot, fly, draw, and write with each device before scoring on our 0–10 scale using Methodology v1.0.

All five picks here are devices we've fully reviewed, ranked by overall score and the creative work each is built for.

## Our Top Picks

### 1. Amazon Kindle Scribe 2 — Best E-Ink Notebook

The Scribe 2 is the first e-ink tablet whose note-taking feels genuinely good — a big, low-glare display, low-latency writing, and software that finally organizes notebooks sensibly. The best device for writers and readers who want paper without the paper. [Read the full review →](/reviews/creator-gear/amazon-kindle-scribe-2/)

### 2. DJI Mini 4 Pro — Best Drone

Under 250 grams (so no FAA registration), folds to fit a jacket pocket, shoots crisp 4K/100fps, and dodges obstacles in every direction. The drone that removed every excuse not to shoot aerials. [Read the full review →](/reviews/creator-gear/dji-mini-4-pro/)

### 3. Peak Design Everyday 30L — Best Camera Bag

A bag engineered, not stitched. The MagLatch closure, flex-fold dividers, and weatherproof shell adapt to a camera kit, a laptop, or a weekend's clothes. The most versatile creator backpack you can buy. [Read the full review →](/reviews/creator-gear/peak-design-everyday-30l/)

### 4. Wacom Cintiq Pro 17 — Best Pen Display

A color-accurate 4K drawing surface paired with Wacom's class-leading Pro Pen 3 — the standard by which every other pen display is judged. The pro choice for illustration, retouching, and design work. [Read the full review →](/reviews/creator-gear/wacom-cintiq-pro-17/)

### 5. Sony a7C II — Best Compact Full-Frame Camera

Full-frame image quality and Sony's industry-leading autofocus crammed into a body you'll actually carry. The a7C II is the travel and street mirrorless that doesn't make you choose between capability and portability. [Read the full review →](/reviews/creator-gear/sony-a7c-ii/)
