## Overview

This workflow enables fitness coaches to use smartwatch motion data (accelerometer, gyroscope) combined with AI to deliver real-time form correction during workouts. By integrating Moov Now, Train AI, and Zapier, you can automate feedback loops and track client progress.

## Prerequisites

- A smartwatch with motion sensors (e.g., Apple Watch, Wear OS, or Moov Now)
- Moov Now app installed on client's phone
- Train AI account (free tier available)
- Zapier account (free tier available)
- Client consent for data collection

## Step 1: Set Up Moov Now for Motion Data Capture

1. **Install Moov Now** on the client's smartphone and pair it with their smartwatch.
2. **Configure workout types** (e.g., squats, deadlifts) in the Moov app. It uses the watch's accelerometer and gyroscope to track movement patterns.
3. **Enable real-time audio coaching** in Moov settings. The app provides verbal cues for form correction based on motion data.

## Step 2: Connect Moov Now to Train AI via Zapier

1. **Create a Zapier account** and create a new Zap.
2. **Set trigger as "New Workout Completed" in Moov Now** (requires Moov Now Zapier integration).
3. **Set action as "Create Exercise Session" in Train AI**.
4. **Map fields**: Map Moov's motion data (e.g., rep count, range of motion, symmetry) to Train AI's session parameters.

## Step 3: Configure Train AI for Form Analysis

1. **Log into Train AI** and create a new AI model for form correction.
2. **Upload reference videos** of correct exercise form (optional but recommended).
3. **Define form rules**: Set thresholds for range of motion, speed, and symmetry based on exercise type.
4. **Enable real-time feedback**: Train AI can send alerts via webhook when form deviations exceed thresholds.

## Step 4: Automate Real-Time Feedback Loop

1. **Create a second Zap** with trigger "New Form Alert" from Train AI (via webhook).
2. **Set action as "Send SMS" or "Send Push Notification"** to the coach's phone using Twilio or Pushover.
3. **Customize message**: Include client name, exercise, and specific correction (e.g., "John: Squat depth too shallow - lower hips").

## Step 5: Monitor and Adjust

1. **Review weekly reports** from Train AI summarizing form issues per client.
2. **Adjust form rules** in Train AI based on client progress.
3. **Provide personalized feedback** during live sessions using the real-time alerts.

## Tips for Success

- **Test with one client** before scaling.
- **Ensure smartwatch is worn correctly** for accurate data.
- **Combine with video analysis** for complex movements.

## Troubleshooting

- **No data in Train AI**: Check Zapier connection and Moov Now permissions.
- **Delayed alerts**: Use webhook instead of polling.
- **False positives**: Adjust sensitivity in Train AI rules.
