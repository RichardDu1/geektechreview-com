## AI-Driven Menstrual Cycle Prediction and Symptom Management Using Wearable Temperature and HRV

This workflow guides women's health specialists in using wearable temperature and HRV data combined with AI to predict menstrual cycles and manage symptoms. By integrating tools like Oura Ring, Natural Cycles, and Clue, you can provide data-driven insights to patients.

### Prerequisites
- Oura Ring (Gen 3 or newer)
- Natural Cycles subscription (FDA-cleared)
- Clue app (free or premium)
- Smartphone with Bluetooth

### Step 1: Set Up Wearable Data Collection
1. **Oura Ring**: Pair with the Oura app. Ensure nightly wear for accurate temperature and HRV readings.
2. **Enable Period Prediction**: In Oura app, go to Settings > Period Prediction and enable it. This uses temperature and HRV to estimate cycle phases.

### Step 2: Integrate with Natural Cycles
1. **Download Natural Cycles** and create an account.
2. **Connect Oura Ring**: In Natural Cycles, go to Settings > Connected Devices > Oura. Authorize data sharing.
3. **Daily Temperature Input**: Natural Cycles will automatically import Oura's temperature data. Confirm daily readings.
4. **AI Prediction**: The app's algorithm uses temperature and cycle history to predict ovulation and fertile windows.

### Step 3: Enhance with Clue App
1. **Download Clue** and set up your profile.
2. **Log Symptoms**: Track symptoms like cramps, mood, flow intensity. Clue uses AI to identify patterns.
3. **Sync Data**: While Clue doesn't directly sync with Oura, you can manually input temperature or use Clue's integration with Apple Health/Google Fit to import HRV.
4. **Review Predictions**: Clue provides cycle predictions based on logged data and AI.

### Step 4: Analyze and Interpret Data
1. **Export Data**: From Natural Cycles, export cycle history (CSV). From Clue, export symptom logs.
2. **Use AI Analysis**: Upload CSV to a tool like **ChatGPT** (with data privacy) to generate insights: e.g., "Identify patterns of PMS severity correlated with HRV dips."
3. **Create Patient Reports**: Summarize cycle length variability, ovulation timing, and symptom trends.

### Step 5: Implement in Clinical Practice
1. **Share Insights**: Provide patients with visual reports from Natural Cycles and Clue.
2. **Adjust Treatment**: Use data to recommend lifestyle changes, medication timing, or further testing.
3. **Monitor Progress**: Have patients continue logging; review monthly.

### Troubleshooting
- **Missing Temperature Data**: Ensure Oura ring is charged and worn consistently.
- **Inaccurate Predictions**: Check for irregular sleep or illness affecting HRV.
- **Data Privacy**: Advise patients to use HIPAA-compliant tools if needed.

### Conclusion
By combining Oura Ring's biometric data with Natural Cycles' FDA-cleared algorithm and Clue's symptom tracking, women's health specialists can offer precise, AI-driven cycle management. This workflow reduces guesswork and empowers patients with actionable insights.
