## Overview

This workflow enables mining geologists to rapidly map and evaluate ore grade across large mining sites using drones and AI. By combining high-resolution orthomosaics, digital elevation models (DEMs), and multispectral data with machine learning models trained on core sample assays, you can generate accurate ore grade maps without extensive drilling.

## Prerequisites

- **Drone**: DJI Mavic 3 Enterprise (RTK module recommended) or similar with RGB and multispectral sensors.
- **Software**: Pix4Dmapper (photogrammetry), CloudCompare (point cloud analysis), GeologicAI Ore Estimator (AI grade estimation).
- **Ground Control Points (GCPs)**: 5-10 per km² for georeferencing.
- **Core Sample Data**: Historical assay data for training AI model.

## Step 1: Flight Planning and Data Acquisition

1. **Define survey area** using QGIS or Google Earth. Export KML boundary.
2. **Plan flight** in DJI Pilot 2 or Pix4Dcapture:
   - Overlap: 80% front, 70% side for photogrammetry.
   - Altitude: 100m AGL for 2-3 cm/pixel resolution.
   - Multispectral: Capture at solar noon for consistent lighting.
3. **Deploy GCPs** with RTK GPS (2 cm accuracy). Record coordinates.
4. **Execute flight** – ensure battery swaps for large areas (20 min per battery).

## Step 2: Photogrammetry Processing

1. **Import images** into Pix4Dmapper. Add GCP coordinates.
2. **Run initial processing** (keypoints extraction, matching).
3. **Optimize** with GCPs – ensure RMSE < 3 cm.
4. **Generate outputs**:
   - Orthomosaic (GeoTIFF)
   - Digital Surface Model (DSM)
   - Point cloud (LAS/LAZ)

## Step 3: Multispectral Analysis

1. **Load multispectral images** into Pix4Dmapper (if separate).
2. **Compute vegetation indices** (NDVI, Iron Oxide ratio) to identify alteration zones.
3. **Export reflectance maps** as GeoTIFFs.

## Step 4: AI Ore Grade Estimation

1. **Prepare training data**:
   - Align core sample assay values (e.g., %Cu) with corresponding pixel locations in orthomosaic.
   - Extract spectral signatures (RGB + multispectral bands) at each sample point.
2. **Train model** in GeologicAI Ore Estimator:
   - Use Random Forest or Neural Network.
   - Input features: band values, elevation, slope, NDVI.
   - Target: ore grade.
   - Split data: 70% train, 30% test.
3. **Validate model** – target R² > 0.85.
4. **Apply model** to entire orthomosaic to generate grade map (GeoTIFF).

## Step 5: 3D Visualization and Volume Calculation

1. **Import point cloud** into CloudCompare.
2. **Classify ground points** using CSF filter.
3. **Generate DEM** from ground points.
4. **Calculate ore volume** by subtracting DEM from DSM and multiplying by grade map.
5. **Export** as shapefile or KML for GIS.

## Step 6: Reporting and Integration

1. **Create report** in QGIS with grade map, volume estimates, and confidence intervals.
2. **Export** to PDF or web map (e.g., using QGIS2Web).
3. **Integrate** with mine planning software (e.g., Surpac, Datamine) via GeoTIFF import.

## Tips and Best Practices

- **Calibrate multispectral sensor** before each flight using reflectance panel.
- **Use RTK drone** to minimize GCPs (1 per 2 km²).
- **Retrain AI model** quarterly with new assay data.
- **Validate grade map** with at least 5 random drill holes per 10 km².

## Expected Outcomes

- **Accuracy**: Grade estimation within 10% of lab assays.
- **Time**: 1 day for 5 km² survey vs. 2 weeks manual.
- **Cost**: $5,000 vs. $50,000 for traditional drilling.
