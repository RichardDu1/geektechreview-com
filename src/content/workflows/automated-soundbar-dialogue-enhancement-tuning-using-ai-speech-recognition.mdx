## Overview

This workflow leverages AI speech recognition to automatically tune your soundbar's dialogue enhancement settings. By analyzing reference audio clips and your room's acoustics, you can achieve studio-quality vocal clarity without manual EQ adjustments.

## Prerequisites

- **Soundbar**: Dolby Atmos compatible (e.g., Sonos Arc, Samsung HW-Q990C)
- **Microphone**: USB measurement mic (e.g., MiniDSP UMIK-1) or your smartphone's mic
- **Software**: Audacity (free), Sonarworks SoundID (trial or paid), and the soundbar's companion app
- **Reference Audio**: A 5-minute clip of dialogue-heavy content (e.g., news podcast, movie scene)

## Step 1: Capture Room Acoustics

1. Place the measurement microphone at your primary listening position (ear height).
2. Open **Audacity** and set the input to your microphone.
3. Play a sine sweep from 20Hz to 20kHz through your soundbar (use a test tone generator).
4. Record the sweep in Audacity. Save the file as `room_sweep.wav`.

## Step 2: Analyze with AI Speech Recognition

1. Open **Sonarworks SoundID** and import `room_sweep.wav`.
2. Select "Dialogue Enhancement" mode. The AI will analyze the frequency response and identify dips/peaks affecting speech clarity.
3. Generate a correction curve. Export it as a parametric EQ preset (e.g., `dialogue_fix.txt`).

## Step 3: Apply EQ to Soundbar

1. Open your soundbar's companion app (e.g., Sonos S2, SmartThings).
2. Navigate to the EQ settings. Most soundbars allow custom 5-10 band EQ.
3. Manually input the parametric EQ values from the exported preset. If your soundbar supports it, upload the file directly.
4. Save the preset as "Dialogue Enhanced".

## Step 4: Validate with Real Content

1. Play your reference audio clip through the soundbar.
2. Use a real-time spectrum analyzer (e.g., Room EQ Wizard) to verify that the speech frequencies (300Hz-4kHz) are now flat.
3. Listen for clarity. Adjust the center frequency of the dialogue band (around 2kHz) by ±1dB if needed.

## Step 5: Automate Future Tuning

1. Create a recurring monthly reminder to re-run the sweep (room acoustics change with furniture, etc.).
2. Use **Sonarworks SoundID**'s auto-calibration feature if available (some versions support real-time correction via a plugin).
3. For advanced users: script the Audacity recording and Sonarworks import using Python and the command line.

## Troubleshooting

- **Muffled dialogue**: Boost 2kHz-4kHz by 2dB.
- **Sibilance (harsh 's' sounds)**: Cut 6kHz-8kHz by 1dB.
- **No EQ control on soundbar**: Use an external DSP like MiniDSP 2x4 HD between source and soundbar.

## Conclusion

With this AI-assisted workflow, you can achieve professional dialogue clarity in under an hour. The process is repeatable and adapts to your room's unique acoustics, ensuring every word is heard.
