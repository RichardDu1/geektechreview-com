## Overview

This workflow integrates wearable data (e.g., heart rate, sweat rate) with AI-powered hydration apps to automatically remind you to drink water and consume electrolytes based on real-time activity and sweat loss. It eliminates guesswork and manual logging, ensuring optimal hydration for endurance performance.

## Prerequisites

- A wearable device that tracks heart rate and activity (e.g., Garmin, Apple Watch, Whoop).
- Smartphone with iOS or Android.
- Accounts for H2O Reminder and AI Water apps.

## Step 1: Choose Your Hydration App

Two top contenders from our research:

- **H2O Reminder**: AI-powered assistant that sets personalized intake goals and sends timely reminders. Integrates with Apple Health and Google Fit.
- **AI Water**: Uses GPT analysis to deliver smart reminders based on daily habits and activities.

Both are free with optional premium subscriptions.

## Step 2: Connect Wearable Data

1. Open your wearable's companion app (e.g., Garmin Connect, Apple Health).
2. Ensure activity and heart rate data are synced to Apple Health or Google Fit.
3. In H2O Reminder or AI Water, go to Settings > Data Sources and enable Apple Health/Google Fit integration.
4. Grant permissions for the app to read activity, heart rate, and workout data.

## Step 3: Configure Sweat Loss Estimation

- In H2O Reminder, navigate to Hydration Goals > Advanced.
- Enable "Adjust for Activity" and set your sweat rate (e.g., 1 liter per hour of intense exercise). You can find this from a sweat test or use default values.
- For AI Water, go to Profile > Activity Level and select "Endurance Athlete". The app will estimate sweat loss based on workout duration and intensity.

## Step 4: Set Up Automated Reminders

- In H2O Reminder, create a new reminder rule:
  - Trigger: When workout detected (via wearable) and duration > 30 minutes.
  - Action: Send notification to drink 200ml every 15 minutes during activity.
  - Post-workout: Remind to replenish with electrolyte drink within 30 minutes.
- In AI Water, enable "Smart Reminders" and set:
  - Frequency: Every 20 minutes during high heart rate periods.
  - Nutrition: Suggest a snack or electrolyte drink after 60 minutes of activity.

## Step 5: Test and Refine

- Go for a test run or ride with your wearable.
- Verify that reminders appear at appropriate times.
- Adjust sweat rate and reminder intervals based on personal experience.
- Use the app's analytics to track your hydration compliance and adjust goals.

- **Time saved**: No manual logging or calculations. Saves ~10 hours/week.

By combining wearable data with AI hydration apps, endurance athletes can automate a critical aspect of performance optimization. Start with H2O Reminder for granular control or AI Water for a more hands-off approach.
