## Overview

Atrial fibrillation (AFib) is a common arrhythmia that often goes undetected. Smartwatches with single-lead ECG capabilities (e.g., Apple Watch Series 4+, Samsung Galaxy Watch) can capture rhythm strips. AI algorithms can analyze these recordings to flag potential AFib episodes. This workflow outlines how to integrate these tools into a clinical setting for efficient, AI-assisted screening.

## Prerequisites

- **Hardware**: Apple Watch Series 4+ (or compatible smartwatch) and/or KardiaMobile device.
- **Software**: Cardiogram app (iOS/Android) or Kardia app by AliveCor.
- **AI Platform**: Cardiogram's DeepHeart AI or KardiaAI (built into Kardia app).
- **EHR Integration**: Epic or Cerner (optional, for seamless data import).
- **Patient Consent**: Ensure patients agree to share ECG data.

## Step 1: Patient Onboarding and Device Setup

1. **Select Patients**: Ideal candidates are those with palpitations, syncope, or risk factors (hypertension, diabetes, age >65).
2. **Provide Device**: Prescribe or recommend a compatible smartwatch or KardiaMobile. Ensure patients know how to record a 30-second ECG.
3. **Install Apps**: Have patients install Cardiogram or Kardia app and create an account.
4. **Connect to AI**: In Cardiogram, enable "AFib Detection" (powered by DeepHeart). In Kardia, the AI analysis is automatic.

## Step 2: Data Collection and AI Analysis

1. **Patient Recording**: Instruct patients to record an ECG when symptomatic or daily at a set time.
2. **AI Processing**: The app's AI analyzes the waveform for AFib patterns.
   - Cardiogram: Provides a probability score (e.g., "AFib likely").
   - KardiaAI: Classifies as "Normal", "AFib", or "Unclassified".
3. **Alerting**: Both apps can send notifications to the patient and optionally to the clinic if configured.

## Step 3: Reviewing AI Results in Clinical Dashboard

1. **Access Dashboard**: Log into Cardiogram's clinician portal or KardiaPro (AliveCor's provider platform).
2. **Review Flags**: Filter patients with AI-detected AFib episodes. View raw ECG strips alongside AI interpretation.
3. **Confirm Diagnosis**: Manually overread the ECG to confirm or reject AI findings. Document in EHR.

## Step 4: Integration with EHR (Optional but Recommended)

1. **Use FHIR APIs**: Cardiogram and KardiaPro support HL7 FHIR for data exchange.
2. **Auto-Import**: Configure EHR to receive AI-flagged ECGs as structured data (e.g., LOINC codes).
3. **Trigger Alerts**: Set up rules in EHR to notify you when a new AFib episode is detected.

## Step 5: Clinical Decision and Follow-up

1. **Confirmatory Testing**: If AI suggests AFib, consider a 24-hour Holter or event monitor.
2. **Treatment**: Initiate anticoagulation or rate control as per guidelines.
3. **Monitor Trends**: Use the AI platform to track AFib burden over time.

## Comparison with Traditional Methods

- **Traditional**: 24-hour Holter monitors are cumbersome and may miss paroxysmal AFib.
- **AI-Smartwatch**: Continuous monitoring, patient-initiated recordings, and AI triage reduce false positives and improve detection rates.

## Troubleshooting

- **Poor Signal Quality**: Ensure electrodes are clean and watch is snug.
- **False Positives**: AI may flag sinus arrhythmia or artifact. Always overread.
- **Data Privacy**: Ensure HIPAA-compliant platforms (Cardiogram and AliveCor are HIPAA-compliant).

## Conclusion

This AI-driven workflow enables cardiologists to efficiently screen for AFib using smartwatch ECGs, reducing manual review time and improving early detection. Start with a pilot group of 10-20 patients to refine the process.
