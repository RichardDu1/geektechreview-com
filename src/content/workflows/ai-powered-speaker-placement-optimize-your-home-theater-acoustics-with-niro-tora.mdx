## AI-Powered Speaker Placement Workflow Guide

### Overview
This workflow uses AI tools to recommend optimal speaker and listener positions based on room acoustics and layout. By analyzing thousands of design combinations, you can achieve superior sound quality without manual trial and error.

### Prerequisites
- Room dimensions (length, width, height)
- Speaker specifications (frequency response, dispersion pattern)
- Listener position preferences
- Access to AI tools: NIRO, TORA, and a free room acoustics calculator

### Step 1: Measure Room Dimensions
Use a laser measure to capture accurate room dimensions. Note any irregularities like alcoves, columns, or sloped ceilings.

### Step 2: Input Data into Room Acoustics Calculator
Go to [Room Acoustics Calculator](https://www.acoustic.ua/forms/calculator.html) (free, physics-based). Enter your room dimensions to get room modes, RT60, and initial speaker/subwoofer placement suggestions. This provides a baseline.

### Step 3: Use NIRO for Speaker and Listener Placement
NIRO (from REDI Acoustics) analyzes thousands of combinations for speaker and listener positions. Input:
- Room geometry
- Speaker models
- Listener area
NIRO outputs optimal positions to minimize low-frequency nulls and peaks.

### Step 4: Refine with TORA for Acoustic Treatment
TORA optimizes placement of acoustic panels, bass traps, and diffusers. It works alongside NIRO to ensure balanced decay time and spatial deviation.

### Step 5: Validate with Real-World Testing
Use a measurement microphone (e.g., MiniDSP UMIK-1) and REW software to verify frequency response at the listening position. Adjust slightly if needed.

### Step 6: Document and Present to Client
Generate a report with before/after graphs, speaker positions, and treatment layout. Use visuals from NIRO/TORA to demonstrate value.

### Tips
- Always account for furniture and room usage (e.g., seating, walkways).
- For subwoofers, use the subwoofer crawl method to confirm AI suggestions.
- Combine with Dolby Atmos guidelines for height channels.

### Conclusion
AI-powered tools like NIRO and TORA reduce guesswork, save hours, and deliver measurable acoustic improvements. This workflow is essential for modern home theater designers.
