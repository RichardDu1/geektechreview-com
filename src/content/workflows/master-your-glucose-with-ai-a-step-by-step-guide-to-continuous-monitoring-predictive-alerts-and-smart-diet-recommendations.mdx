## Introduction

Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) with AI predictive alerts and diet recommendations is a game-changer for diabetes management. This guide walks you through setting up a complete system using real-world tools.

## Step 1: Choose Your CGM Device

Select a CGM that offers real-time data and AI integration. Top options:

- **Dexcom G6**: No fingerstick calibration, 10-day sensor, real-time alerts.
- **Abbott Freestyle Libre 3**: 14-day sensor, small form factor.
- **Medtronic Guardian 4**: Requires calibration but integrates with insulin pumps.

**Recommendation**: Dexcom G6 for best AI compatibility.

## Step 2: Set Up the CGM Sensor

1. Clean the application site (abdomen or upper arm) with alcohol.
2. Insert the sensor using the applicator per manufacturer instructions.
3. Pair the sensor with the receiver or smartphone app (e.g., Dexcom G6 app).
4. Wait for the 2-hour warm-up period.

## Step 3: Enable AI Predictive Alerts

Use apps that leverage machine learning to predict glucose trends:

- **Sugarmate**: Syncs with Dexcom and provides predictive alerts via Apple Watch, CarPlay, and smart displays. Set thresholds for low/high predictions.
- **Gluroo**: Offers customizable predictive alerts and shares data with caregivers.

**Configuration**:
- In Sugarmate, go to Settings > Alerts > Predictive Low/High. Set prediction time (e.g., 20 minutes).
- Enable urgent low soon alert (predicted < 55 mg/dL within 20 min).

## Step 4: Integrate Diet Recommendations

Pair your CGM with an AI-driven nutrition platform:

- **Nutrisense**: Provides personalized meal recommendations based on your glucose responses. Upload food logs and get real-time feedback.
- **Levels**: Offers a food scoring system and insights on how meals affect your glucose.

**Setup**:
- Create an account on Nutrisense and connect your Dexcom account.
- Log meals via the app or use the photo-based food recognition.
- Review the AI-generated recommendations (e.g., “Swap white rice for quinoa to reduce spike”).

## Step 5: Automate Workflows with IFTTT or Zapier

Create automations to act on alerts:

- **IFTTT**: Connect Dexcom to smart lights (e.g., flash red when low).
- **Zapier**: Log glucose readings to Google Sheets or send SMS to your doctor.

**Example Zap**:
- Trigger: New Dexcom reading < 70 mg/dL
- Action: Send email to caregiver with glucose value and time.

## Step 6: Monitor and Adjust

Review weekly reports from Sugarmate or Nutrisense to identify patterns. Adjust diet, exercise, or medication based on AI insights.

## Conclusion

By combining a CGM with AI predictive alerts and diet recommendations, you can proactively manage your glucose levels, reduce anxiety, and improve quality of life. Start with the tools above and customize to your needs.
