## Overview

This workflow guides you through setting up an automated wind turbine blade inspection system using a drone equipped with high-resolution cameras and deep learning software for crack detection. By following these steps, you can reduce inspection time by up to 80% and detect cracks earlier.

## Prerequisites

- DJI Matrice 300 RTK or similar industrial drone with 30x zoom camera
- SkyVisor or Blade³.ai subscription
- Tablet or laptop for mission planning
- Safety gear (helmet, harness) for on-site work

## Step 1: Mission Planning

1. Open SkyVisor or Blade³.ai on your device.
2. Import turbine coordinates or select from map.
3. Set flight parameters: altitude 20m, speed 2m/s, overlap 80%.
4. Define inspection zones (leading edge, trailing edge, tip).
5. Save mission as template for repeat inspections.

## Step 2: Pre-Flight Checks

- Verify drone battery >80% and SD card formatted.
- Check weather: wind <25km/h, no rain, visibility >5km.
- Ensure no obstacles near turbine.
- Calibrate compass and IMU.

## Step 3: Execute Automated Flight

1. Launch drone from safe location (50m from turbine).
2. Start mission; drone will autonomously orbit and capture images.
3. Monitor live feed for anomalies.
4. After completion, drone returns to home point.

## Step 4: Data Upload and AI Analysis

1. Remove SD card and transfer images to computer.
2. Upload to SkyVisor or Blade³.ai cloud platform.
3. Select "Deep Learning Crack Detection" analysis.
4. Wait for processing (typically 10-30 minutes per turbine).

## Step 5: Review Results

1. Open generated report: highlights cracks, erosion, and delamination.
2. Filter by severity (critical, major, minor).
3. View annotated images with bounding boxes and crack length.
4. Compare with previous inspections to track progression.

## Step 6: Generate Maintenance Report

1. Export PDF report with images and recommendations.
2. Include repair priority list and estimated downtime.
3. Share with maintenance team via email or CMMS.

## Tips for Best Results

- Inspect during low wind and good lighting (overcast is ideal).
- Use infrared camera for subsurface defects.
- Calibrate AI model with local blade types for higher accuracy.

## Troubleshooting

- **Blurry images**: Reduce drone speed or increase shutter speed.
- **GPS loss**: Enable RTK for precise positioning.
- **False positives**: Adjust confidence threshold in AI settings.
