### Speaker Impedance Calculator
A free web tool that helps you compute total impedance when wiring multiple speakers. Enter individual impedances and wiring configuration (series, parallel, or series-parallel) to get the total load. Also provides power distribution and compatibility warnings. Essential for any home theater builder.
