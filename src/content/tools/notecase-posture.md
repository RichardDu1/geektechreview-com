The Notecase Posture wearable is a compact, clip-on device that streams accelerometer and gyroscope data via Bluetooth Low Energy. It has a 12-hour battery life and is comfortable for all-day wear. The open API allows easy integration with Edge Impulse and other AI tools. Build quality is solid, and the companion app provides basic posture tracking out of the box. Compared to similar devices like the Upright Go, the Notecase offers raw data access, making it ideal for custom AI workflows.
