IFTTT (If This Then That) is a no-code automation service that lets you connect your favorite apps and devices. For this workflow, it bridges Sleep as Android with smart lights or speakers. The free tier allows up to 3 applets, which is sufficient for basic wake-up routines. The Pro version adds multi-step applets and faster execution. Setup is straightforward: choose a trigger (e.g., alarm goes off) and an action (e.g., turn on lights). IFTTT is reliable and supports hundreds of services, making it a versatile tool for personal automation.
