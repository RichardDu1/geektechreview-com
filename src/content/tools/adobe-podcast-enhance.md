Adobe Podcast Enhance is a free web-based tool that uses AI to clean up audio recordings. It excels at reducing background noise and reverb, making it perfect for pre-processing legacy surround tracks. Simply upload a stereo WAV/MP3, and within minutes you get a polished file ready for spatial upscaling. No account required for basic use.
