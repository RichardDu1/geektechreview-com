Auphonic is a versatile AI audio tool that offers automatic leveling, noise reduction, and spatial audio upmixing. Its 'Spatial Audio' algorithm can upmix stereo to 5.1 or 7.1 with adjustable width and center channel. The free tier is generous for hobbyists, and the web interface is intuitive. For batch processing, the desktop app is available.
