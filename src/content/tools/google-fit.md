Google Fit provides a centralized dashboard for biometric data from Wear OS smartwatches. It offers a Stress score based on heart rate and activity. Zapier has a direct integration, making it easy to trigger automations when stress levels exceed thresholds. The platform is free and works with many Android smartwatches.
