Sleep as Android is the gold standard for sleep tracking on Android. It supports a wide range of wearables and uses advanced algorithms to detect sleep phases. The smart alarm feature is its killer app: it wakes you during light sleep within a configurable window, reducing morning grogginess. The app also offers lullabies, sleep recording, and integration with IFTTT and Tasker for smart home automation. While the free version is functional, the premium unlocks additional features like sleep debt tracking and advanced statistics. Highly recommended for anyone serious about optimizing their sleep.
