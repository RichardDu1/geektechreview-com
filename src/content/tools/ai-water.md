AI Water leverages GPT to analyze your activity patterns and provide adaptive hydration reminders. It automatically estimates sweat loss based on your activity level (e.g., endurance athlete) and workout intensity. The app also suggests nutrition (electrolytes, snacks) after prolonged exercise. Its interface is simple and intuitive, making it great for athletes who prefer a set-and-forget solution.
