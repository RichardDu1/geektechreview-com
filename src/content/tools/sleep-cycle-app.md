Sleep Cycle uses your phone's microphone and accelerometer to track sleep stages and wake you during light sleep. It provides sleep quality graphs, snore detection, and trend analysis. Data can be exported as CSV. It's a cost-effective alternative to wearables, though less accurate for deep sleep measurement.
