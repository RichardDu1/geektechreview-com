Clue is a popular period tracking app that uses AI to predict cycles and identify symptom patterns. It allows detailed logging of over 30 symptoms, including mood, pain, and flow. The app's algorithm improves over time, offering personalized insights. While it doesn't natively integrate with Oura, it syncs with Apple Health for HRV data. Compared to Natural Cycles, Clue is more comprehensive for symptom management but less precise for ovulation. Real-world usability: intuitive design, but predictions can be less accurate without consistent logging. Premium adds advanced analytics.
