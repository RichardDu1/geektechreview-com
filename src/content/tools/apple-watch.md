The Apple Watch Series 8 and Ultra offer continuous HRV monitoring, which is a key metric for stress detection. The watchOS Health app integrates seamlessly with third-party services via HealthKit. For stress management, the watch can also detect elevated heart rates and prompt breathing exercises natively, but for automated triggers, external automation is needed.
