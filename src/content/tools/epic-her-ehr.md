Epic is one of the most widely used EHR systems. It supports HL7 FHIR for importing patient-generated health data, including ECG recordings from smartwatch apps. By integrating with Cardiogram or KardiaPro, cardiologists can automatically receive AI-flagged ECGs into the patient's chart, streamlining workflow and ensuring data is in one place.
