Google Home (now Nest) speakers are excellent for creating a gradual wake-up experience. Paired with IFTTT, you can have it play a morning playlist or read the news when your smart alarm goes off. The sound quality is good for its size, and it integrates seamlessly with Google services. For this workflow, it acts as the audio output for your alarm, providing a gentle audio cue. Alternatively, you can use any smart speaker with IFTTT support, such as Amazon Echo.
