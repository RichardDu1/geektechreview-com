The Empatica EmbracePlus is a research-grade wearable that provides continuous physiological monitoring. It captures electrodermal activity (EDA), photoplethysmography (PPG), skin temperature, and actigraphy. It is FDA-cleared and used in clinical studies. For fatigue detection, it can identify stress and sleep patterns, feeding data into custom AI algorithms.
