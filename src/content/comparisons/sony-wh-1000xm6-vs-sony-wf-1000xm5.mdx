## The Big Question

Same brand, same ANC pedigree, two very different form factors. The **Sony WH-1000XM6** are full-size over-ear headphones; the **WF-1000XM5** are true wireless earbuds. This isn't really a question of which is "better" — it's which fits your life. Headphones give you more ANC and battery; earbuds give you portability and discretion. Here's the breakdown to help you decide.

## Spec Comparison

| Spec | Sony WH-1000XM6 (Headphones) | Sony WF-1000XM5 (Earbuds) |
|---|---|---|
| Form factor | Over-ear, full size | True wireless earbuds |
| ANC | Best-in-class | Class-leading for earbuds |
| Battery | 30 hrs (ANC on) | 8 hrs + 16 in case (24 total) |
| Portability | Bulky, non-folding | Pocketable case |
| Sound | Warm, full, detailed | Warm, surprisingly full |
| Comfort | All-day (heavier) | In-ear fit dependent |
| Workout-friendly | No | Yes (sweat resistance) |
| Price | $399 | $299 |
| GeekTechReview Rating | 8.5 | 9.8 |

## Performance Comparison

**ANC** — the over-ear XM6 still blocks more noise, full stop. Larger ear cups and drivers simply seal out the world better than earbuds can. That said, the WF-1000XM5 earbuds close the gap dramatically — they're among the best ANC earbuds you can buy and easily handle commutes and gyms.

**Sound** — the headphones have the edge in fullness and soundstage, with deeper, more controlled bass and more detail. But the earbuds punch well above their size; you may be surprised how full they sound. For critical listening, headphones; for casual listening, the earbuds are plenty.

**Battery** — headphones win on endurance: 30 hours of ANC versus the earbuds' 8 hours per charge (24 with the case). But earbuds recharge fast in the case, so in practice both get you through a day.

## Portability & Use Case

This is the real deciding factor. The **earbuds** disappear into a pocket, work for workouts, and don't mess up your hair or get in the way of a hat — they're the everyday, everywhere option. The **headphones** are bulky and not pocketable, but they're far more comfortable for long flights and home/office sessions, and they double as a better home-listening device.

## Value Comparison

The earbuds are $100 cheaper and, given how good their ANC and sound have become, arguably the smarter purchase for most people. The headphones earn their premium if you want the best possible ANC, longest battery, and serious listening quality. Our testing scored the earbuds a remarkable 9.8 — they're that good for the form factor.

## The Verdict

**Tie — by design.** If you want one device for flights, long sessions, and home listening with the best ANC, get the **WH-1000XM6**. If you want one device for commuting, workouts, and everyday life that fits in your pocket, get the **WF-1000XM5**. Many people will ultimately want both — but if forced to pick one, the earbuds' versatility and our 9.8 score make them the more universally useful choice.
