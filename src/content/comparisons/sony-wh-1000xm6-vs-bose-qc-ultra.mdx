## The Big Question

When it comes to premium over-ear noise-canceling headphones, two names dominate: **Sony's WH-1000XM6** and **Bose's QuietComfort Ultra**. Both deliver flagship ANC, plush comfort, and excellent sound — but they prioritize different things. Sony leans into raw noise-blocking and battery; Bose leans into wear-it-all-day comfort and immersive spatial audio. Here's how they actually compare.

## Spec Comparison

| Spec | Sony WH-1000XM6 | Bose QuietComfort Ultra |
|---|---|---|
| ANC | Best-in-class (QN1e chip) | Excellent, slightly behind Sony |
| Battery | 30 hrs (ANC on) | ~24 hrs (ANC on) |
| Comfort | Very good, slightly heavier | Best-in-class, featherlight clamp |
| Sound | Warm, bass-forward, detailed | Neutral, spacious |
| Spatial Audio | Basic | Immersive mode (standout) |
| Multipoint | Yes, improved | Yes |
| Folding | Non-folding | Non-folding |
| Price | $399 | $429 |
| GeekTechReview Rating | 8.5 | 8.0 |

## Performance Comparison

**Noise cancellation** goes to Sony. The XM6's QN1e chip still silences everything from engine drone to office chatter better than anything else, with a transparency mode that's notably free of the pressure feeling older models had. Bose is a close second — superb on low-frequency rumble, marginally less effective on mid-range voice.

**Sound** is a preference call. Sony is warm and bass-forward with good detail — fun and engaging, less analytical. Bose is more neutral and spacious, with a soundstage that pairs beautifully with its immersive spatial-audio mode. Audiophiles may prefer Bose's tuning; most listeners will enjoy Sony's.

**Battery** is a clear Sony win: 30 hours with ANC on versus Bose's ~24. Over a week of commuting that difference adds up.

## Comfort Comparison

This is Bose's headline strength. The QC Ultra is the most comfortable over-ear headphone you can wear for hours — a light, even clamp and plush cushions that disappear on your head. Sony is very comfortable but a touch heavier; for 6+ hour sessions, Bose wins.

## Value Comparison

Sony is the better value: $399 vs $429, with longer battery and stronger ANC. You're paying Bose's premium for best-in-class comfort and the spatial-audio experience. If those matter most to you, the Bose premium is worth it; otherwise Sony gives you more for less.

## The Verdict

**Winner: Sony WH-1000XM6** — the best all-round ANC headphone, with class-leading noise cancellation, longer battery, and a lower price. Buy the **Bose QuietComfort Ultra** if you prioritize all-day comfort above all or you love the immersive spatial-audio mode. We scored Sony 8.5 and Bose 8.0 — close, but the Sony's combination of ANC, battery, and value tips it.
