The Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is the phone that admits the Note branding is gone for good and leans hard into "AI phone" marketing. Underneath the buzzwords, it's a titanium-frame Snapdragon 8 Elite flagship with a 200MP camera and the S-Pen. It is very good. It is also $1,299.

## Design

Titanium frame (Grade 2), Gorilla Armor glass front that's 4x more scratch-resistant and noticeably less reflective than last year's glass. The flat slab aesthetic continues — sharper corners than the S24 Ultra's micro-curved screen, which polarizes. Weight is 218g. IP68. The S-Pen is integrated in the bottom-right corner, unchanged in capability.

## Performance

Snapdragon 8 Elite (3nm) — a big leap in efficiency and single-core. Paired with 12GB RAM (16GB on 1TB SKU) and UFS 4.0 storage. Geekbench 6: 3,200 single, 10,500 multi. Sustained performance is better than the S24 Ultra thanks to a larger vapor chamber — Genshin Impact at max settings holds 60fps for 30 minutes before throttling to ~50. The phone gets warm but not hot.

## Display

6.9-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 3120×1440, 1-120Hz adaptive, 2,600 nits peak HDR. Anti-reflective Gorilla Armor makes it the most readable phone screen in direct sun. Color accuracy is excellent (ΔE < 1 in Natural mode). PWM dimming at 240Hz down to 10% brightness — flicker-sensitive users may notice; everyone else won't.

## Camera

200MP main (f/1.7, OIS), 50MP 5x periscope tele (f/3.4, OIS), 10MP 3x tele (f/2.4), 50MP ultrawide (f/1.9). The new 50MP 5x is the meaningful upgrade — sharper, brighter, faster focus than the S24U's 50MP 5x. 8K/30 video from the main sensor. The "ProVisual Engine" AI pipeline does heavy noise reduction — sometimes too heavy, smoothing texture in low light. Astrophotography and Expert RAW give you the raw tools if you want them.

## Galaxy AI

Now baked across the OS: circle-to-search, live translation in calls, generative photo editing, summarization in Samsung Notes and browser. Most features require a Samsung account and ship free "until end of 2026" — Samsung is keeping the paid-tier option open. Some of it is genuinely useful (translation, summarization); some is party-trick fluff.

## What's wrong

- $1,299 base. Android flagships are not a bargain anymore.
- The flat-slab corners dig into palms. Cases mandatory.
- Charging caps at 45W — fine, but Chinese competitors hit 100W+.
- One UI 7 is heavy, preloaded with bloat (Facebook, etc.), and pushes Samsung services relentlessly.
- S-Pen functionality has barely evolved in 5 years.
- 7 years of OS updates promised — impressive, but batteries don't last 7 years.

## The verdict

The S25 Ultra is the best Android phone you can buy if you want the kitchen-sink flagship. The camera is excellent, the screen is the best in class, and the AI features range from useful to gimmicky. If you want a cleaner Android experience, the Pixel 10 Pro is more thoughtful. If you want the most, the S25 Ultra is the most.
